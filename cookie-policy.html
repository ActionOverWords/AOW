Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how ActionOverWords Index ("AOW Index," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies when you visit this site — and, just as importantly, what we don't use them for. It works alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers the account and message information we collect more broadly; this page goes deeper on the storage-and-tracking side specifically.

What we mean by "cookies" here

Strictly speaking, this site doesn't set traditional server-side cookies at all today — there's no Set-Cookie header involved anywhere. What it uses instead is browser storage: small pieces of data that JavaScript running on the page reads and writes directly to your own browser, on your own device. Privacy law (and most people's expectations) treat these the same way, so this policy covers both under the umbrella term "cookies," the way most cookie policies do — and we're specific about which is actually in play where it matters.

This is also a young, actively developed project (our Privacy Policy and Terms say the same thing about other parts of the site). If the storage mechanism behind the scenes changes as the site grows, what's described below is what governs — this page gets updated the day that happens, not quietly after the fact.

What's actually in use

Here's the complete list. If a category isn't below, it isn't happening.

CategoryStatusWhat it's for
Strictly necessary Active One item, referenced in the code as aow_session, remembers which account is logged in on this specific browser. It isn't shared with other visitors, isn't used to track you across other sites, and isn't linked to any advertising profile — because there isn't one. Clearing your browser's site data simply logs you out; nothing else on the site breaks. This can't be turned off, because there's no way to keep you signed in without it — the same way there's no version of "stay logged in" that doesn't remember who you are somehow.
Preferences Not separate Things like your display name and dark-mode setting are account preferences stored in the site's own data store once you're signed in — not a separate browser cookie. They travel with your account, not with your browser, so switching devices doesn't lose them.
Analytics Not in use We don't run Google Analytics, Plausible, or any other analytics service. The consent banner's "Analytics" toggle is there so a future, privacy-friendly option can be added without redesigning the banner — but right now, whether you switch it on or off, nothing loads. We'll update this page and the banner the same day that changes.
Advertising Never No ad network, no ad-tracking pixel, no cross-site identifier. This isn't a setting we've turned off — there's no advertising infrastructure on this site to turn on in the first place.

Third-party services

Google Fonts loads the typeface used across this site. Doing so shares your IP address and browser information with Google, the same as it would on most sites that use a hosted font rather than one bundled with the page — Google's own privacy policy governs that exchange, not ours. It loads on every page, including this one.

Payment processor pages — currently whichever is linked from the Donate tab, such as Stripe, PayPal, Ko-fi, or GiveButter — are outside sites we don't control. Whatever cookies or storage they use happen entirely on their own pages, under their own cookie and privacy policies, once you click through to donate or subscribe. We never see those cookies, and nothing from those pages is set on this site.

Any official's photo shown on the site is loaded from wherever that image is hosted; we haven't audited every possible photo host's own tracking practices the way we have our own code, though none is used specifically to track visitors of this site.

How to control this

Change your Analytics preference anytime — right now this only records a choice, since nothing is currently gated behind it, but it's the same control that'll take effect if that ever changes.

Because the "strictly necessary" item can't be disabled without logging you out, there's no toggle for it — that's true of virtually every site that lets you have an account. If you'd rather not have even that, the fix is the same as with any cookie: don't create an account, or clear your browser's site data for this domain, which logs you out immediately.

Your browser also has its own controls — most let you block or delete site storage globally or per-site, and browser extensions can block third-party requests like the one to Google Fonts. Blocking Google Fonts specifically will make the site fall back to your device's default font; it won't break anything else.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as the site's storage and tracking actually change — not on a schedule, but the moment something listed above stops being accurate. The date at the top will change whenever we do. Given how actively this project is still being built (see the caveats in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service), that's more likely to mean a real backend replacing today's prototype storage than it is to mean new tracking — but either way, this is where it gets disclosed.
