  Then: the tab appears for everyone, the plans table on Support Us gets its two new rows
  ("Member votes: one vote per ballot" and "Member forums"), and every promise gets a small
  "Discuss in the member forums" link. "Back to preview" turns it off again; nothing is deleted.

WHO CAN DO WHAT (the server checks all of this, not just the page)
  Signed out: sees vote results. Forums need a free account.
  Free: reads the forums. Plus: reads, and votes. Premium: also replies and marks posts Helpful.
  Supporter: also starts threads. Every paid plan gets the same one vote per ballot.
  Anyone signed in can Report a post; 3 reports from different members hide it until you look.
  Members post under a forum name they pick, never their email or display name.
  Counts on the page are rounded down with a plus (1,234 votes shows as 1,200+).
